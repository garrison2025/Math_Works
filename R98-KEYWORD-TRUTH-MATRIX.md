## Indexation / GEO launch decision

1. **Existing 51 v18 generator URLs:** continue exact-SHA digital math, worked-step, PDF, Chromium and Firefox checks; public index requires the existing explicit route approval and owned-domain gates.
2. **New four preview pages:** useful and reachable, but historically HOLD, strictly `noindex,nofollow`, without canonical/structured data/sitemap authorization. Do not promote merely because they have a real HTTP 200 response.
3. **Any future expansion to unsupported HOLD phrases:** add the **actual mathematical skill**, independent answer and step oracles, downloadable student/teacher PDFs, browser checks, exact-source term alignment and explicit indexing owner approval. No invented Google traffic claims.
4. **Domain/GSC:** separate from R98 engineering and owner-waived offline print/teacher signatures; no purchased domain or verified GSC release in the currently recorded project state.
